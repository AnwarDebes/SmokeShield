The model

SmokeShield is a well-mixed indoor air-quality balance. The PM2.5 concentration inside a home of volume V obeys

V dC_in/dt = (lambda * P * V) * C_out               ← infiltration brings particles in
           - (lambda + k_dep + eta * Q_hvac * D) * V * C_in   ← ventilation, deposition, filtration remove them

where lambda is the air-change rate (1/h), P is the PM2.5 envelope penetration factor (0-1), k_dep is the surface deposition rate (typically 0.2-0.3 /h for PM2.5), eta is the HVAC filter efficiency (ASHRAE 52.2 MERV-rating dependent), Q_hvac is the HVAC recirculation rate (ACH), and D is the HVAC duty cycle (0-1).

At constant outdoor concentration, the steady-state indoor concentration is

C_in_ss = (lambda * P * C_out) / (lambda + k_dep + eta * Q_hvac * D)

For piecewise-constant outdoor concentration, the transient is solved analytically per piece. All computation happens locally in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

The numbers

  • MERV filter efficiencies: ASHRAE 52.2-2017 E1 size range (0.3 - 1.0 µm), interpolated from Consumer Reports 2025 testing and AAQR 2021 measurements.
  • Home archetypes: NCCEH 2023 residential infiltration review and ACS ES&T Air 2024 university wildfire testbed.
  • EPA AQI breakpoints: 2024 revised PM2.5 NAAQS (Annual 9.0 µg/m³, 24-h 35 µg/m³).
  • Relative mortality risk: GBD HRAPIE log-linear concentration-response function, RR = exp(0.0067 (C - 5)) per Burnett et al. 2018 PNAS.

What it is not

SmokeShield is a public-domain decision-support tool. It is not a medical device, and the predicted indoor PM2.5 is a model output informed by your inputs - not a direct measurement. For real-time indoor measurements, an inexpensive optical PM sensor (e.g. PurpleAir, AirGradient) is the gold standard.

Privacy

SmokeShield runs entirely in your browser. When you click “Save my home”, your profile is stored in your browser's localStorage and never leaves your device. There is no account, no server-side data, no analytics, no cookies.
